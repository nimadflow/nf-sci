For the intended interoperable/open-standard strategy, the owner needs to choose and publish the actual licensing terms. A possible division to evaluate is Apache-2.0 for code and CC BY 4.0 for documentation, with evidence/media rights considered separately. This is a proposed choice, not the license of this bundle. CoSAI has its own contribution/CLA process; submitting there is a separate owner-controlled action.

Until that decision is made, call this a **public-review research capsule**, not an adopted open standard or an OSI-licensed release. No inference about the legal identity of the rights holder is made here.
